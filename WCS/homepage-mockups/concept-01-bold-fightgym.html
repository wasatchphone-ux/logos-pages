⬭ For Beginners

No experience needed. Everyone starts somewhere. Here's what to expect:

  • First class: We teach fundamentals. You'll learn proper stance, footwork, and basic combinations. No judgment—only progress.
  • Expect muscle soreness. Combat training is real work. This fades as your body adapts.
  • Bring water and an open mind. Wear comfortable athletic clothes and bring hand wraps if you have them (we can provide).
  • Train at your own pace. Intensity is up to you. Nobody's timing you—focus on technique first.
  • Most beginners see results in 3-4 weeks. Better cardio, clearer mind, stronger foundation.
  • Community is real here. Veterans help new people every single day. You're not training alone.

⬭ FAQ

Do I need prior experience?
No. Our beginner classes start with fundamentals. Everyone trains at their own level.
What should I bring?
Workout clothes, water bottle, and towel. Hand wraps are included or you can bring your own. We have extra gloves available.
How often should I train?
That's up to you. Beginners typically start 2-3 times per week. Serious competitors train 5-6 days. Find your rhythm.
Can I attend drop-in classes?
Yes. Membership gives you full access to all classes. No advance booking needed—show up and train.
