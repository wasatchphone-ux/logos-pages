Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect in my first week of training?
Your first week focuses on fundamentals: proper stance, basic footwork, and foundational techniques. You'll learn how to wrap your hands, the correct way to throw a punch or kick, and how to breathe during training. Soreness is normal and fades within days as your body adapts to the new demands. Our coaches will scale the intensity to your fitness level.
Do I need any prior experience?
No. Everyone starts as a beginner. Our curriculum is designed to teach fundamentals from the ground up, regardless of your background or fitness level. We've trained athletes from college wrestlers to people who've never exercised before.
What equipment should I bring?
Wear comfortable athletic clothes. Bring water and a towel. For hand protection, bring hand wraps (or we provide them) and gloves (or borrow ours). That's it. No special equipment needed to start.
How often should I train?
That depends on your goals. Beginners typically see good fitness results training 2-3 times per week. Serious competitors train 5-6 days. Start with what fits your schedule and increase from there.
Is it intimidating if I'm not athletic?
The first class can feel intimidating, but our community is genuinely welcoming. Every person in the gym has been a beginner. Experienced members actively help new students. You'll feel at home quickly.
