Your First Steps

What to Expect as a Beginner

  • First class: We teach you proper stance and fundamentals. No complicated combos—just the essentials to get started.
  • You'll be sore. That's normal and a sign you worked hard. Most people feel amazing after a few days.
  • Bring athletic clothes and water. We provide hand wraps and gloves (bring your own if you prefer).
  • Train at your own pace. The person next to you isn't your competition—you're training together.
  • Community is real. Experienced members genuinely help newcomers. You'll make friends fast.
  • See progress quickly. Better fitness, clearer mind, real confidence—usually within 3-4 weeks.
  • Tell us what interests you. We will answer questions and share current program details.

Questions? We Have Answers

Do I need to have trained before?
Not at all. Everyone starts as a beginner. Our classes are structured so you learn fundamentals first. Most people with zero experience feel comfortable from day one.
What should I bring to class?
Workout clothes, water bottle, and a towel. We provide hand wraps and gloves, but you can bring your own if you prefer. Just show up ready to work.
How often should I come?
That's totally up to you. Beginners see great results with 2-3 classes per week. Serious people train more. Find a rhythm that works for your life.
Are classes always full?
Nope. Drop in whenever you can. Your membership gives you unlimited access to all classes—no booking required. Just show up.
Is it intimidating for new people?
Some new students feel nervous at first, but Wasatch is genuinely welcoming. Our coaches and members actively help beginners. You'll feel at home quickly.
