Independent Codex design study · Orem, Utah

Four different ways into the gym.

Each concept changes the browsing experience, hierarchy and conversion path. These are not cosmetic variants. All four use real Wasatch imagery and a permanent lead-capture path.

01

Inside the Rounds

Full-screen documentary storytelling for visitors who need to feel the gym before choosing a program.
02

Training Atlas

A fixed navigation rail and dense directory for people who want answers quickly.
03

Program Mosaic

A visual-first gallery where programs are the homepage and details expand in place.
04

Start Command

An above-the-fold lead path built for decisive visitors and campaign traffic.
