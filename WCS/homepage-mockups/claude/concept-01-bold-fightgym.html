FAQ

Do I need prior experience?
No. Our beginner classes start with fundamentals. Everyone trains at their own level.
What should I bring?
Workout clothes, water, and towel. Hand wraps included. We have extra gloves available.
How often should I train?
Beginners: 2-3 times per week. Competitors: 5-6 days. Find your rhythm.
Can I drop in?
Yes. Your membership gives full access. No advance booking needed.
Is it intimidating?
Some feel nervous at first. Our community is genuinely welcoming. You'll feel at home quickly.
First class free?
Yes. No commitment. Come try it before deciding.
