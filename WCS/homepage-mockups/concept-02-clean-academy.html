For Beginners

No prior experience required. Here's what to expect when you start training:

  • First class focuses on fundamental stance, footwork, and basic techniques.
  • You'll be sore after training. This is normal and fades within a few days.
  • Bring athletic clothes, water, and towel. Hand wraps and gloves provided.
  • Train at your own intensity. We teach proper form first, intensity comes later.
  • Most beginners notice improved fitness and confidence within 3-4 weeks.
  • Our community actively helps new students. You're never training alone.
  • Contact us with your program interest and questions. We will respond with current options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience to start?
No. Our beginner classes teach fundamentals from the ground up. Everyone starts at the beginning regardless of background.
What should I bring to my first class?
Workout clothes, water bottle, and towel. Hand wraps and gloves are provided or you can bring your own. We have extra equipment available.
How often should I train?
That depends on your goals. Beginners typically see good results training 2-3 times per week. Serious competitors train 5-6 days.
Can I drop in to classes?
Yes. Your membership gives you unlimited access to all classes. No need to book in advance—just show up when you can.
What's the difference between levels?
Beginner classes focus on fundamentals and form. Intermediate classes add complexity and intensity. Advanced classes prepare competitive students.
