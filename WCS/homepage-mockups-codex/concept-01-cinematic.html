Before the first bell.

Do I need experience to start Muay Thai in Orem?

No. Wasatch welcomes first-time students. Expect controlled fundamentals, clear coaching and a conversation about the right class before advanced work.

Which Wasatch program is best for a complete beginner?

That depends on what you want from training. Muay Thai and boxing emphasize striking, Jiu Jitsu emphasizes grappling, and Strength & Conditioning supports general athletic development. A coach can help you choose.

Are youth martial arts classes separated by age?

Yes. Youth Juniors and Youth Seniors follow distinct schedules and developmental paths rather than sharing one catch-all kids class.
