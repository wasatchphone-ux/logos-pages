Decision FAQ

Questions before contact.

Which combat sport should a beginner choose?

Choose by the movement and goal that interest you. Muay Thai and boxing focus on striking, Jiu Jitsu focuses on grappling, MMA connects ranges, and Strength & Conditioning builds the athletic base. Wasatch can help you narrow the fit.

Does Wasatch have separate youth age groups?

Yes. Youth Juniors and Youth Seniors are distinct paths, with forty-five minute weekday time blocks designed for different stages.

When can working adults train in Orem?

The schedule includes early morning and evening classes, with current times ranging from around 6 a.m. through 9 p.m. depending on the day and program.
