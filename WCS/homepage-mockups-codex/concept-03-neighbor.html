Good questions

Before you walk in.

Is Wasatch Combat Sports friendly to complete beginners?

Yes. Beginners are expected to learn the room and the fundamentals before intensity. Tell the coach you are new and what brought you in.

Does Wasatch have martial arts classes for young children and teens?

Yes. Youth Juniors and Youth Seniors follow distinct age-based paths rather than one combined Youth Program experience.

Can women train in a dedicated class?

Yes. Women's Combat appears on the weekday schedule, alongside the gym's broader Muay Thai, boxing, Jiu Jitsu and MMA options.

Where is Wasatch Combat Sports located in Orem?

The gym is at 972 N 1430 W, Orem, UT 84057. The single Orem location serves families and adults across Utah County.
