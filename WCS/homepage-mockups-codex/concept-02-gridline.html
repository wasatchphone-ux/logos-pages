02 / Beginner protocol

Start at zero.
Build from there.

You are not expected to know the stance, the language or the room. The first class is orientation through movement. A coach shows you where to stand, what to work and how to progress safely.

  1. Tell us the goal. Fitness, self-defense, confidence or competition all point to different starting lines.
  2. Learn the structure. Warm-up, instruction, controlled practice and class-specific work.
  3. Control the pace. Technique comes before intensity, especially on day one.
  4. Ask the basic question. Clear questions are part of training, not evidence you are behind.

07 / FAQ

Common queries

What martial arts classes are available in Orem, Utah?

Wasatch offers Muay Thai, MMA, Jiu Jitsu, boxing, Women's Combat, Youth Program classes, and Strength & Conditioning at one Orem location.

Can a total beginner start Jiu Jitsu at Wasatch?

Yes. Beginners can start with fundamentals, controlled practice and coaching that explains the room before expecting advanced movement or live work.

What time do classes run at Wasatch Combat Sports?

The current schedule starts around 6 a.m. on weekdays and offers morning, afternoon and evening options, with some classes ending around 9 p.m.

Are Youth Juniors and Youth Seniors separate?

Yes. Younger Juniors and older Seniors follow distinct age-based paths rather than one combined kids class.

Where is the gym located?
