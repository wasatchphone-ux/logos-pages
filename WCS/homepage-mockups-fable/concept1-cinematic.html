Before you ask

Do I need experience to start Muay Thai in Orem?
No. Muay Thai 101 and Jiu-Jitsu 101 are standing weekly classes built for people who have never trained. Most of our members walked in with zero experience.
What should I bring to my first class?
Workout clothes and water. That's it — we'll sort gear out as you go, and you won't need gloves or a gi on day one.
What age can my child start?
Kids' classes start as young as four. The youth program is split into Juniors (4:00 PM) and Seniors (4:45 PM) so your child trains with kids their own age and size — we'll help you pick the right group.
Will I have to spar?
No. Sparring is opt-in, gear-checked and coach-supervised. Beginners drill technique — nobody gets thrown to the wolves.
Where are you located and when are classes?
972 N 1430 W, Orem, UT 84057 — one location serving all of Utah County. Classes run roughly 6 AM to 9 PM weekdays, plus Saturday mornings.
