Your first week, honestly.

No secret handshake. Here's exactly how it goes.

What actually happens:

  1. Show up 15 minutes early in gym clothes. Bring water. That's the whole packing list — no gloves or gi needed yet.
  2. Start in a 101 class. Muay Thai 101 and Jiu-Jitsu 101 run every week — real classes on the real schedule, full of other beginners.
  3. Learn stance, footwork and your first techniques with partners at your level while a coach walks the room.
  4. Nobody makes you spar. Sparring is opt-in, gear-checked and supervised — most folks wait weeks, and that's exactly right.
p.s. — the hardest part is walking in the door. After that, we've got you.

Fair questions, honest answers.

The five we hear most at the front desk.

Do I need experience to start Muay Thai in Orem?
Nope. Muay Thai 101 and Jiu-Jitsu 101 run every single week — real beginner classes full of other first-timers. Most of our members started with zero experience.
What should I bring to my first class?
Gym clothes and a water bottle. No gloves, no gi, no gear on day one — we'll help you figure out equipment once you know you love it.
What age can my child start?
As young as four. Kids split into Juniors (4:00 PM) and Seniors (4:45 PM) so they train with kids their own age and size — come watch a class first, most parents do.
Will I have to spar?
Never on day one, and never without asking for it. Sparring is opt-in, gear-checked and coach-supervised. Technique comes first, always.
Where exactly are you in Orem?
972 N 1430 W, Orem, UT 84057 — just off I-15, an easy drive from Provo, Lindon, Vineyard and anywhere in Utah County.
