FAQ

Answers before you have to ask.

The questions people type into Google before their first class.

Do I need experience to start Muay Thai in Orem?
No — Muay Thai 101 and Jiu-Jitsu 101 run every week specifically for people who have never trained. Most members started at zero.
What should I bring to my first class?
Gym clothes and water. No gloves, gi, or gear needed on day one.
What age can my child start martial arts?
From age 4. Kids split into Juniors (4:00 PM) and Seniors (4:45 PM) by age and size — and you're welcome to watch first.
Will I have to spar?
No. Sparring is opt-in, gear-checked, and coach-supervised. Beginners drill technique first — for weeks, if that's your pace.
Where is Wasatch Combat Sports located?
972 N 1430 W, Orem, UT 84057 — just off I-15, minutes from Provo, Lindon and Vineyard.
What happens after I send the form?
A coach calls or texts you, answers questions, and books your first class. No card, no contract — it starts a conversation.
