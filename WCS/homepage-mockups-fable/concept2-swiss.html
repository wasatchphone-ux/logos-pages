Why Wasatch

One location. One standard.

The case, in plain terms

Wasatch Combat Sports is Utah County's premier combat studio — one gym in Orem where every discipline runs under one roof and one coaching standard.

The rating isn't a slogan. It's 338 five-star Google reviews from the people on the mat — parents, first-timers and competitors. The coaches who competed at the highest levels teach the 6 AM class and the kids' class with the same care.

  • Black-belt and pro-veteran led programs, every discipline
  • Standing 101 classes for true beginners — on the schedule weekly
  • Youth split by age: Juniors and Seniors train separately
  • Women-only program five mornings a week

First session protocol

No experience required
STEP 001

Arrive T-minus 15

Show up 15 minutes early in workout clothes. No gear needed. No contract talk at the door.

STEP 002

Enter the 101

Muay Thai 101 and Jiu-Jitsu 101 run every week. Real beginner classes, on the timetable above.

STEP 003

Drill the basics

Stance, footwork, first techniques — partnered at your level while coaches walk the room.

STEP 004

Sparring: opt-in only

Nobody spars day one. You ask when you're ready; coaches gear-check and supervise every round.

07

Asked & answered

Schema-ready Q&A
Q-01Do I need experience to start Muay Thai in Orem?+
No. Muay Thai 101 and Jiu-Jitsu 101 are standing weekly classes built for people who have never trained. Most members started with zero experience.
Q-02What should I bring to my first class?+
Workout clothes and water. No gloves, no gi, no gear required on day one — we sort that out as you go.
Q-03What age can my child start?+
Kids start as young as four. Juniors train at 4:00 PM, Seniors at 4:45 PM — grouped by age and size, and we help you pick the right one.
Q-04Will I have to spar?+
No. Sparring is opt-in, gear-checked and coach-supervised. Beginners drill technique first.
Q-05Where are you located?+
972 N 1430 W, Orem, UT 84057 — just off I-15, serving Orem, Provo and all of Utah County.
